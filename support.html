Common questions

Is my number a medical prediction?

No. LifeMAX is an estimate to think with, built from population life tables and published research — not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a prediction. Real medical questions belong with a doctor.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, and we never see, collect, or sell anything you log. Details in the privacy policy.

My number moved less than I expected. Why?

By design. Nothing jumps in LifeMAX — the clock drifts toward where your habits point at a bounded pace, about a day or two of life per real day. Quick fixes don't exist in the research, so they don't exist in the app.

Can I start over?

Yes — Settings, then Start over. It wipes your baseline, milestones, and journal on that device. There's no penalty; the questionnaire simply runs again.

How do UV alerts work?

Turn them on in Settings under Reminders. On mornings the UV index near you is forecast to hit 7 or higher, LifeMAX pings you around 8:00, scheduled ahead through Apple Weather, so it works without opening the app. Your approximate location goes only to Apple's weather service.

Will my data survive deleting the app or a new phone?

Yes. In Settings, under Backup, Export saves your whole log as a file you keep in Files or iCloud Drive. On a fresh install, tap "Have a backup? Restore it" on the intro screen and pick the file. Backups from older versions always restore into newer ones.
