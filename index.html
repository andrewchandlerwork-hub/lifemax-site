One clock. Five ways it moves.

The Clock

A live count of your weeks, built from actuarial life tables and published research. Your baseline freezes on day one, so you always see how far you've come.

The Journal

Thirty seconds a night: sleep, diet, mood, one win. A weekly check-in moves your habits. Streaks and achievements keep you honest.

Milestones

Commit to quitting smoking and watch the years walk back, day by day. Take on stress, sleep, or diet. Each one earns time.

The Receipts

Every factor links to the study behind it. No jargon. We tell you what the research actually means for you.

The Wrist

Apple Watch app and complications keep the clock in sight. Apple Health fills in what your devices already know.
