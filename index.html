LifeMAX Privacy Policy

Effective: July 4, 2026

LifeMAX is built around one rule: your data stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. LifeMAX has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no trackers. Everything the app knows about you lives only on your device:

- Your questionnaire answers, life estimate, journal entries, milestones, and medication log are stored in the app's local storage on your phone. - If you connect Apple Health, LifeMAX reads steps, workouts, sleep, height, and weight on your device to adjust your habit levels. Health data is read-only, is never written back, and never leaves your phone. - Your live score is shared with the LifeMAX widget through Apple's App Group storage, which is private to the app and stays on the device.

What we never do

- We never transmit, upload, or store your data anywhere outside your phone. - We never sell, share, or monetize your data in any form. Apple's HealthKit rules forbid using Health data for advertising, marketing, or sale, and we follow that rule for all of your data, Health or not. - We never track you across apps or websites. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

What leaves your phone, and when

Only what you send yourself. The "Report a problem" button opens your own email app with a draft addressed to us; you see exactly what it contains and choose whether to send it. Links in the Research tab open studies in your browser. That's the complete list.

Deleting your data

Settings, then Start over, wipes everything the app stores. Deleting the app does the same. If you connected Apple Health, you can revoke access anytime in iOS Settings, then Health, then Data Access & Devices.

Not medical advice

LifeMAX turns population statistics into a rough personal estimate. It is a tool for thinking about time, not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a prediction. Medical questions belong with a clinician.

Children

LifeMAX is not directed at children under 13, and the intake requires an age of 13 or older.

Changes

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy and the app will say so plainly before the change applies to you.
