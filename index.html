The average long life is about 4,000 weeks. Most of us can name our bank balance to the dollar — and our remaining time not even to the decade.

Answer 21 honest questions. Get a personal estimate built from actuarial life tables and published research. Then watch it respond to how you actually live.

One clock. Five ways it moves.

The Clock

A live count of your weeks. Your baseline freezes on day one, so you always see how far you've come. Nothing jumps — days earn days.

The Journal

Thirty seconds a night: sleep, diet, mood, one win. A weekly check-in moves your habits. Streaks and achievements keep you honest.

Milestones

Commit to quitting smoking and watch the years walk back, day by day. Take on stress, sleep, or diet. Each one earns time.

The Receipts

Every factor links to the study behind it. No jargon — we tell you what the research actually means for you.

Apple Watch + Health

Complications keep the clock in sight. Apple Health fills in what your devices already know — sleep arrives logged.

Widgets

Your weeks on the home screen and lock screen, alternating with the goal you're chasing.
